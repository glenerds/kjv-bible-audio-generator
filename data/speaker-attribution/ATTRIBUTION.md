The build-preparation script downloads the pinned MACULA Quotation and Speaker Data source and its original `LICENSE.md`, then writes this notice into the application bundle:

“MACULA Quotation and Speaker Data, © 2023 by Clear Bible, Inc, available at https://github.com/Clear-Bible/speaker-quotations/.” Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

The source repository says some character-detail records contain FCBH / Glyssen Character Detail material, copyright (c) 2014–2020 SIL LSDev and Faith Comes By Hearing, licensed under the MIT License. The build preparation script preserves the MIT notice separately as `LICENSES/GLYSSEN_MIT.txt`.

The dataset is not KJV-specific. Never describe its verse/speaker suggestions as authoritative KJV quotation attributions. It must be reviewed against the imported KJV text.
