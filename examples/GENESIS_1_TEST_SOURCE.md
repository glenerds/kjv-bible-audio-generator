# Genesis 1 test text source

`genesis_1_kjv_test.txt` contains the 31 verses of Genesis 1 in public-domain King James Version wording. It is included only as a repeatable full-chapter parser/pipeline fixture, not as the full Bible text and not as a substitute for the user's chosen import.

Source: Cyber Library, *The Bible, King James version*, Book 1: Genesis, Chapter 1. The source page labels the text public domain and credits the eBook compilation to David Widger with contributions from Derek Andrew (January 1992) and Bryan Taylor (November 2002):

https://www.cyberlibrary.org/en/books/the-bible-king-james-version-book-1-genesis-8001/chapters/1/

The fixture preserves the KJV verse wording as displayed on that source page. No source audio is included or reused. Audio in automated tests is generated by the program's explicitly test-only synthetic backend.
